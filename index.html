How the model works

  • Recommended selections add the PV and BESS design margins to the minimum sizes, then round up to whole modules and battery units. The battery figures already include DOD and efficiency.
  • The battery must also cover morning and evening hours when solar output is below the load. The array qualifies once its surplus on the design day can put that full amount back into the battery.
  • Changes from the Excel: day-tariff hours are 13 h throughout (Sheet1 D29 says 11). Non-TOU daytime units are spread per hour, where Sheet2 G19 added the daily figure to an hourly rate. DOD is now applied to the battery result.
